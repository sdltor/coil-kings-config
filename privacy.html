Coil Kings: Snake Arena — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-11-26 · App ID: com.newgame.coilkings · Contact: privacy@newgame.studio

Short version: Coil Kings does not track you across other apps or websites, includes zero third-party advertising SDKs, and never collects your name, email, contacts, photos, or location. Optional gameplay analytics default to OFF.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Coil Kings is offline-first and stores your game progress and preferences on your device using standard platform storage (PlayerPrefs / NSUserDefaults / app-private files):

  • Match progress, coins, emerald gems, unlocked skins, scale dyes, dioramas, bestiary stamps, honors, and daily streaks (HMAC integrity-sealed locally).
  • Audio, graphics, colorblind mode, reduce motion, text scale, and control/gamepad settings.
  • Cached signed Remote Config (remote_config.json) so mode and safety switches keep working while offline.

Tracking & ATT: Coil Kings does not request App Tracking Transparency (NSPrivacyTracking: false) because it performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

3. Data We Never Collect

  • No player-typed names or squad names are ever sent off your device.
  • No email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precise GPS location, camera, microphone, or photos.
  • No hardware serials or advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID).
  • No third-party data sharing or sale for advertising.

4. Retention

  • Local save data: Stays on your device until you reset progress or uninstall the app.
  • Crash & opt-in telemetry events: Retained for at most 90 days and then automatically purged.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

  • Turn off analytics anytime: Open Settings → About in Coil Kings and switch Anonymous analytics off.
  • Reset your anonymous ID: Tap Settings → About → Reset ID to revoke consent and immediately rotate to a fresh random installId.
  • Delete local save data: Tap Settings → Reset Progress or uninstall Coil Kings.
  • Request server-side deletion: Email privacy@newgame.studio with the anonymous installId shown when long-pressing the Coil Kings logo on the Home screen. Deletion requests are fulfilled within 30 days.
